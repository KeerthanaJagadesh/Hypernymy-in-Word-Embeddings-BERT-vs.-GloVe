HYPERNYMY IN WORD EMBEDDINGS - BERT VS. GLOVE: 
A controlled comparison of a contextual embedding model (BERT) and a static embedding model (GloVe) on whether word embeddings encode hypernymy — the asymmetric "is-a" relation between a specific concept and its general category (e.g. poodle → dog). Both models are evaluated on 514 hyponym–hypernym pairs extracted from WordNet's animal domain under identical data and evaluation conditions.
